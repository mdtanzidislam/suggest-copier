Privacy Policy – Suggest Copier

Last updated: May 2025

1. Overview

Suggest Copier is a Chrome browser extension that displays Google autocomplete suggestions in a convenient side panel and allows users to copy them to their clipboard. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

Suggest Copier does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your search queries.
  • We do not collect the suggestions shown in the panel.
  • We do not collect browsing history.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not send any data to external servers.

3. How the Extension Works

The extension reads autocomplete suggestion text that Google already renders on the page (within your browser's DOM). This data never leaves your device. The clipboard permission is used only when you click "Copy" or "Copy All" — it writes text to your local clipboard only.

4. Permissions Used

  • clipboardWrite — to copy suggestion text to your clipboard when you click the copy button.
  • host_permissions (google.com) — to inject the suggestion panel into Google search pages.

5. Third Parties

This extension does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the new version will be published with the extension update on the Chrome Web Store.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via the Chrome Web Store support page for this extension.
